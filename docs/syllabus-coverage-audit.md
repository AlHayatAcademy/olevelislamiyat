# Syllabus Coverage Audit

Living document. Tracks how much of the official Cambridge O Level Islamiyat 2058 (and parallel IGCSE 0493) syllabus is represented on the site, and where content is still placeholder.

## Exam pattern (extracted from `01-syllabus/Cambridge_O_Level_2058.pdf`, Section 3–4)

- Two written papers, answered in English, both required for a grade. Grades A*–E.
- **Paper 1** — 1.5 hours, 50 marks, 5 questions; must answer Q1, Q2, and 2 others.
  1. Major themes of the Qur'an — 3 passages set (from Appendix 1), candidate chooses 2 — 8 marks
  2. History and importance of the Qur'an — essay-length, multi-part — 14 marks
  3. Life and importance of the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
  4. The first Islamic community — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
- **Paper 2** — 1.5 hours, 50 marks, 5 questions; must answer Q1, Q2, and 2 others.
  1. Major teachings in the Hadiths of the Prophet — 4 passages set (Appendix 2), candidate chooses 2 — 8 marks
  2. History and importance of the Hadiths — essay-length, multi-part — 14 marks
  3. Rightly Guided Caliphs and their importance as leaders — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
  4. The Articles of Faith and the Pillars of Islam — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
- AO1 (Recall, select, present relevant facts): 68% weighting. AO2 (Understanding significance): 32% weighting.
- Levels-of-response mark grids extracted in full (Q1 part a: 0–4; Q2–5 part a: 0–10; part b AO2: 0–4).

### Verification log (no fabricated facts) — updated Milestone 5

- **Paper 1 Major Themes lessons** (9 lessons): built from the site founder's own note images (`source/09-notes/P1/theme 1.png`, `theme 2.png`, `theme 3.png`), which already carry English translations attributed to specific verse references. Per the instruction not to fabricate Qur'an translations, the exact Arabic text/translation of every passage is **not** reproduced verbatim from the note image in the site's lesson content; every lesson instead states the reference and gives an original thematic explanation, with an explicit `[VERIFY: Arabic text and translation to be added from a verified Qur'an translation]` placeholder marking where the exact quotation needs checking against a verified published translation (e.g. Saheeh International, Abdel Haleem) before being used as a citable quotation in revision materials.
- **Paper 1 "Arabia Before Islam" lesson**: built directly from `source/09-notes/P1/Arabia.png` (headings, structure, and the two Qur'anic references it cites — 12:40 and 107:1-3 — used by reference only, not reproduced as a direct quotation from the note image).
- **Paper 1 "First Islamic Community" lessons** (6 lessons): no dedicated note-image set exists for this section; content is drawn from general, well-established Seerah and companion-history facts (names, family relationships, well-documented roles) treated the same way Umar's (RA) caliphate lesson was treated last session — safe general knowledge, not textbook paraphrase. Flagged here in case the user wants a source image added for full traceability.
- **Paper 2 Major Teachings of Hadith lessons** (8 lessons): built from the site founder's own note images (`source/09-notes/P2/Teaching of Hadith Individual.png`, `Teaching of Hadith communal.png`, `Teaching of Hadith brotherhood.png`). Exact Hadith wording is **not** reproduced verbatim from the note images in the site's lesson content; every lesson instead names the teaching and the collection generally associated with it and gives an original explanation, with an explicit `[VERIFY: Hadith reference and exact wording to be added from an authenticated collection]` placeholder marking where exact wording needs checking against an authenticated Hadith collection before citation. Qur'anic verses referenced on the same note images (59:9, 49:11-13, 3:110, 5:2, 21:92) are cited by reference only, not quoted verbatim from the notes.
- **Paper 2 Articles of Faith & Pillars lessons** (11 lessons): no note-image set exists for this section; content is drawn from the public syllabus scope and general, well-established Islamic-studies knowledge (the six Articles of Faith and five Pillars are standard, uncontested doctrinal content), cross-referenced to Paper 1 Qur'an passages and Paper 2 Hadith teachings already authored with their own citations.
- **Uthman and Ali (RA) caliph lessons**: no note image exists for either in `source/09-notes/P2` (an "Abu Bakr RA election.png" note exists but was used for the earlier Abu Bakr lesson only). Content relies on well-established, uncontested facts of early Islamic history (shura election process, Qur'anic standardisation, Battle of the Camel, Battle of Siffin, the Khawarij, both assassinations) — the same treatment as Umar's (RA) lesson.
- No Hadith numbers, Qur'an translations, or dates were invented this session. Every place where exact scriptural quotation would normally appear and could not be verified against a checked source is explicitly marked with a `[VERIFY: ...]` placeholder rather than filled with plausible-sounding text.

### Coverage summary after Milestone 5

- **All 8 official syllabus sections (4 per paper) now have full lesson content** — every section hub shows only "available" subtopics; none remain entirely "coming soon".
- Paper 1: 24 lessons live. Paper 2: 28 lessons live. 52 lessons total, plus 8 section hub pages = 60 dynamic content routes (verified in the Next.js build output — see `docs/build-status.md`).
- **Still an honest gap, not claimed as done**: the syllabus's Appendix 1 (15 set Qur'an passages) and Appendix 2 (4 set Hadith passage-groups) are covered by a strong *representative* selection (9 of the 15 Qur'an passages across all 3 themes; 8 Hadith teachings across both groups) rather than exhaustive passage-by-passage / Hadith-by-Hadith pages for every single prescribed item. A future pass could extend each theme/group from its current 3-4 representative lessons to full coverage of every syllabus-listed passage, if the user wants that level of completionism.
- Every `[VERIFY: ...]` placeholder introduced needs a verified published Qur'an translation or authenticated Hadith collection lookup before the exact wording is filled in and the placeholder removed.

# Syllabus Coverage Audit

Living document. Tracks how much of the official Cambridge O Level Islamiyat 2058 (and parallel IGCSE 0493) syllabus is represented on the site, and where content is still placeholder.

## Exam pattern (extracted from `01-syllabus/Cambridge_O_Level_2058.pdf`, Section 3–4)

- Two written papers, answered in English, both required for a grade. Grades A*–E.
- **Paper 1** — 1.5 hours, 50 marks, 5 questions; must answer Q1, Q2, and 2 others.
  1. Major themes of the Qur'an — 3 passages set (from Appendix 1), candidate chooses 2 — 8 marks
  2. History and importance of the Qur'an — essay-length, multi-part — 14 marks
  3. Life and importance of the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
  4. The first Islamic community — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
- **Paper 2** — 1.5 hours, 50 marks, 5 questions; must answer Q1, Q2, and 2 others.
  1. Major teachings in the Hadiths of the Prophet — 4 passages set (Appendix 2), candidate chooses 2 — 8 marks
  2. History and importance of the Hadiths — essay-length, multi-part — 14 marks
  3. Rightly Guided Caliphs and their importance as leaders — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
  4. The Articles of Faith and the Pillars of Islam — 1–2 questions, multi-part — 14 marks
- AO1 (Recall, select, present relevant facts): 68% weighting. AO2 (Understanding significance): 32% weighting.
- Levels-of-response mark grids extracted in full (Q1 part a: 0–4; Q2–5 part a: 0–10; part b AO2: 0–4).
